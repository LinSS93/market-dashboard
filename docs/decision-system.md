# Decision-system boundaries

The stock monitor owns the only live decision contract. Radar / 机会雷达, laboratory replay, company profiles, and provider summaries are research aids. 它们不是第二套技术评分，也不能将研究优先级翻译为买卖指令。

Research context may delay a new positive entry while evidence is incomplete, but it must not weaken `TRIM/EXIT` or hide an existing risk condition. 研究上下文不得弱化 `TRIM/EXIT`，也不得隐藏既有风险条件。User verification remains required for every investment decision.
